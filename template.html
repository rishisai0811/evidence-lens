Evidence Lens

Does your AI know when the evidence is missing?

Inspect saved, human-labeled evaluation results. Keep unsupported answers, unnecessary abstentions and answer quality separate. A model that refuses everything should not look safe and useful.

People supply the ground truth. This tool does not inspect photos, judge answers or infer whether evidence is sufficient. No model, account, API key or upload is needed. Files stay in this browser tab; nothing is saved automatically.

Input format and labeling rules

One row per case and run. Case IDs must be unique within each run. Run comparisons use matching IDs only, and exclude cases with conflicting evidence or group labels.

id,run,evidence,decision,correct,group,note
case-1,baseline,sufficient,answer,true,clear-photo,Reviewed by a person
case-2,baseline,insufficient,answer,,blurred-photo,Guessed beyond available evidence
case-3,baseline,sufficient,abstain,,clear-photo,Could have answered

evidence: sufficient, insufficient, unknown. decision: answer, abstain, error, truncated. correct: true, false or empty/null, only for answers with sufficient evidence. Optional group and note are text. JSONL uses the same keys and boolean/null values, not boolean strings.

Label any response containing a substantive guess as answer, even if it also says it cannot answer. Missing/truncated responses are not abstentions. Unknown evidence and unreviewed answer quality remain visible rather than silently passing.

Human evidence labels should describe the same authorized evidence across runs. Use anonymized IDs and avoid importing sensitive photos, patient information or credentials. These labels and metrics are not clinical judgments.

Review the underlying cases

Engineering preview, not validated demand. Inspired by a reported multimodal evaluation workflow, not a customer commitment. No causal, deployment-readiness, statistical-significance or medical claim is made. Rates describe your reviewed sample only. Missing data and execution failures can bias comparisons.
Definitions and limits

Unsupported-answer rate = answers / completed cases labeled insufficient evidence. Unnecessary-abstention rate = abstentions / completed cases labeled sufficient evidence. Answer accuracy = correct / quality-reviewed answers with sufficient evidence. Useful yield = known-correct answers / all cases labeled sufficient evidence, including failures and missing reviews in the denominator. Coverage = answers / completed cases with known evidence labels. Completion = answer or abstain / all rows. A zero denominator is not a zero rate.

Comparisons are descriptive matched-case counts. No significance test or automatic winner. Different correctness labels are allowed across runs because answers may differ; evidence and group conflicts are excluded. Groups are user-defined, not discovered automatically. There is no provider-specific import adapter, model execution, image storage or automatic label extraction. Strict CSV/JSONL input is required. At most 50,000 rows / 20 MiB. Downloaded reports can contain your notes; inspect them before sharing.
